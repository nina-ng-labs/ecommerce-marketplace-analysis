# Ecommerce Marketplace Analysis — Olist

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Brazilian Olist ecommerce marketplace to understand GMV growth, category contribution, seller concentration, customer retention, and key business drivers.

The analysis uses SQL for data exploration and Power BI for visualization.

## Business Questions

1. How has GMV changed over time?
2. Which product categories contribute the most GMV?
3. Which sellers generate the most revenue?
4. What percentage of customers are repeat customers?
5. What are the primary drivers of GMV growth?

## Key Findings

- GMV growth was primarily driven by a strong increase in order volume.
- From January to February 2017, orders increased by 120.4%, while AOV decreased by 4.9%.
- Repeat customers account for only around 3% of customers, indicating strong reliance on new customer acquisition.
- The top 10 categories contribute approximately 63.27% of total GMV.
- The largest category contributes only 9.45%, meaning no single category dominates marketplace sales.
- The top 10 sellers contribute only 13.29% of GMV, showing low seller concentration.

## Driver Decomposition

GMV growth was decomposed into:

GMV = Orders × AOV

The investigation showed that order volume was the main growth driver, while AOV slightly declined.

Further analysis showed:

- Customer growth is heavily dependent on new customers.
- Category contribution is diversified.
- Seller revenue is highly distributed across the marketplace.
